Gods Lies

A pigpen cipher drawn from fourteen small gods, each with their own way of lying — mix any two grid-shaped ciphers with any two diamond-shaped ciphers into your own private alphabet, then write and read in symbols.

1Choose your cipher

Click a slot below, then click a name's tile to fill it — filling one slot jumps you to the next automatically (Grid·1 → Grid·2 → Diamond·1 → Diamond·2, then stops). The colored corner on a tile shows every slot it's already filling. You can also click a slot directly to target it, then click any name's tile to fill just that one. This key is used for writing only — Read (other tab) has its own, so you can write and read with different keys.
